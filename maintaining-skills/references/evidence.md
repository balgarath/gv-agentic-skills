# Evidence behind the maintaining-skills rules

The figures the entry file's rules rest on. The entry file keeps the rule and points here. Tags: `provenance-tags.md`. Source for the research figures below and for the claims tagged ASSERTED 2026-10-27 (recurrence gating, pruning units being whole skills, no study on executable docs, repetition biasing which value an agent follows): a research note in the original author's working files, not shipped. No web source was re-read, so none is tagged above ASSERTED.

- **The eight rot modes** were found in a 3,309-line production skill, the blender and art-direction corpus, which was inside every stated size limit at the time (OBSERVED 2026-10-27).
- **Why the merge pass is human-gated:** curated skills gain ~+16pp, self-generated ones about nothing (SkillsBench, ASSERTED 2026-10-27, one benchmark read through a summarising fetch).
- **Why pruning is capped:** over-pruning is a measured harm. A harsh retirement threshold turned a large gain into a loss in the one controlled study (Library Drift, an unreplicated ICML 2026 workshop paper; ASSERTED 2026-10-27).
- **Why the audit looks for repeated helpers:** Cycles device setup was rewritten in 8 scripts on one project and 2 on another (OBSERVED(cylinder-mirror) and OBSERVED(festival-scene), counted 2026-11-04); a contact-sheet helper was written seven times before `art-direction/scripts/contact_sheet.py` (OBSERVED(art-direction), 2026-11-08).
- **Why a reviewer reads the diff after the scripts pass:** 2026-11-03 blender pass, six defects with both checkers passing, two made by the pass. 2026-11-04 pass, 13 defects then 4 more in the fixes. 2026-11-05 Triangle City pass, a diff rejected with every script passing; the worst defect was an exception that lost its "Standard view, one city" scope on the way into the entry file. 2026-11-05 self-merge, 2 HIGH, 4 MEDIUM, 11 LOW, the HIGHs in procedure written that day. 2026-11-08 blender and art-direction merge, 5 MEDIUM and 11 LOW, then 1 MEDIUM and 2 LOW in the fixes. 2026-11-08 strict project move, 4 MEDIUM and 9 LOW with every script passing, among them two prose pointers to files the pass had emptied and an allowlist widened to make the guard pass (OBSERVED(blender, art-direction, maintaining-skills), six passes).
- **Why the diff read stops at two reads:** on the 2026-11-04 pass the last edits were committed with only the scripts re-run, and the fixes had already made 4 defects. On the 2026-11-05 pass the second read found only LOWs; on both 2026-11-08 passes the fix read found nothing above MEDIUM and the pass ended there (OBSERVED, four passes). The earlier version of this stopping rule was promoted by an owner decision, short of the recurrence bar (2026-11-05).
- **Why firm rules go to the person as questions, quoted in full:** the 2026-11-05 Triangle City pass of 69 entries asked nothing. "Never raise emission" had passed three audits and two exception rounds on its evidence, and the owner then overruled the ban itself, since tuning by eye is how the owner works. Those audits checked scope, not whether an imperative matched the user's practice (OBSERVED(blender), one pass, the owner's stated preference).
- **Why the rest is admitted without a question:** the question count grew with the procedure, not with the risk. The 2026-11-05 self-merge of 8 entries took five question rounds. The 2026-11-08 blender merge asked 14 numbered questions for 35 entries, nearly all answered "as recommended". The two self-audits of 2026-11-08 produced 19 questions for 13 entries, while four held entries had been re-judged and re-confirmed on three successive passes with no new evidence (OBSERVED(maintaining-skills), merge logs 2026-10-28 to 2026-11-08).
- **Why process lessons need two passes before entering SKILL.md:** the entry file grew from 221 lines and 13.8 KB (2026-10-27) to 320 lines and 21.2 KB (2026-11-08) across six passes, and all eleven entries in its own intake on 2026-11-08 were about its own process (OBSERVED(maintaining-skills), `git log`).
- **Why the routing check exists:** an agent reads a big reference in pieces, so routing decides whether a fact is found. On the 2026-11-05 Triangle City pass the 3x culling route could not be reached from the "render is slow" row until a reviewer added it (OBSERVED(blender)). The ~300-line bound is skill-creator's table-of-contents bound, adopted by an owner decision (HEURISTIC 2026-11-05).
- **Why binary facts are re-run before admission:** on the 2026-10-28 merge, 3 of 13 entries' accounts proved wrong when re-checked (OBSERVED(triangle-battle)). A worker's claim about which Blender was first on PATH went into a brief unverified and was wrong; the command that settled it was `which -a` or `Get-Command`.
- **Why a DOC page is named only after opening it:** the 2026-11-03 pass upgraded about 28 bare `[DOC]` tags to named pages by topic without opening any, which looked verified and was not (OBSERVED(blender)).
- **Why new files are checked against git after apply:** an unanchored ignore pattern such as `projects/` can match every new `skills/*/projects/` folder and a commit would drop them silently; anchor such patterns.
- **Why body size is not a defect:** skill-creator is a healthy skill at 485 lines behind one description (OBSERVED(skill-creator), re-counted 2026-11-04).
- **Why a pass checks for shadowed project skills:** a personal skill shadows a project skill of the same name (DOC, code.claude.com/docs/en/skills, opened 2026-11-08). Project copies of `session-wrapup` existed beside the personal one, and one held a deploy risk check that never ran (OBSERVED(session-wrapup)).
- **Process lesson, resuming workers:** in the 2026-11-08 redesign, one apply worker and one diff reviewer were resumed across four rounds with short briefs and nothing retyped (OBSERVED(maintaining-skills), 2026-11-08). An earlier case saw one builder resumed across seven cycles re-pay context (586k tokens). A second case: the 2026-11-04 merge of 29 entries resumed its worker for the apply and fix rounds and the reviewer for the re-check, about 440k tokens for the worker across three rounds, with re-check results and corrected wording kept without retyping (OBSERVED(maintaining-skills), 2026-11-04). Round count or context size may explain the difference. Implementer and reviewer stay separate agents.
- **Process lesson, walking new steps:** when a merge edits the merge procedure itself, walk the new steps in order against that merge before review. On the 2026-11-05 self-merge the reviewer's 2 HIGH defects were both in text written that session (OBSERVED(maintaining-skills), 2026-11-05).
- **Process lesson, hand-built counts:** a Sonnet worker's hand-built intake count said 14 held where `intake_list.py` said 17 (OBSERVED(maintaining-skills), 2026-11-08). SKILL.md already requires the script's output.
- **Process lesson, reworded values:** an apply worker reworded dates and numbers in new REVIEW.md files to pass the value guard (OBSERVED(maintaining-skills), 2026-11-08). The reviewer found each legitimate, but the move can blur a real figure; `rot-modes.md` rot 2 states the rule.
